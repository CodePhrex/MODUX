# MODUX — Official Brand Guidelines

**Status:** Approved visual direction (November 2026)  
**Brand:** MODUX  
**Developer:** CodePhrex  
**Tagline:** Modify Your Universe.

## Identity
Premium futuristic Android customization studio with a dark sci-fi aesthetic, luminous accents, rounded cards, subtle glass-like surfaces and fluid animation.

### Logo
Hexagonal outline containing a stylized capital **M**, using a cyan-to-ultraviolet gradient. Maintain clear space and preserve proportions. Do not substitute another emblem.

### Approved colors
| Token | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight | `#0B1021` | Primary background |
| Electric cyan | `#45E5F9` | Highlights and active controls |
| Ultraviolet | `#985AFF` | Gradients and primary actions |
| Neon pink | `#FF4CA8` | Secondary accents |
| Snow white | `#F5F6FC` | Text and contrast |

### Typography
- **Orbitron:** headings, branding and promotional assets
- **Inter:** navigation, controls, body copy and documentation

Fonts must be licensed and sourced separately; do not commit font files without verifying redistribution rights.

## Approved screen concepts
1. App icon: hexagonal M on dark rounded-square background
2. Splash screen: logo, wordmark, tagline, glowing abstract wallpaper
3. Dashboard: Wallpapers, Effects, Icons, Keyboards, Lighting/LEDs, Typography, Widgets, Home Screens, Colors
4. Theme browser: searchable theme cards and category filters
5. Theme creator: preview, wallpaper/icon/color/font/effect tabs and apply action

The approved image mockup is a visual reference, not proof of implemented functionality. Theme download counts, featured examples and device status text in mockups are illustrative only.

## Asset handling
Place approved original exports in `assets/brand/` and `assets/mockups/`. Prefer lossless PNG or SVG where available. Do not recreate or replace the approved graphics without review.
